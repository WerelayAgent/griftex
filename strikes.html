SAN VERANO · THE PICKET LINES

San Verano's strikes.

Once a city week the staff of each venue decide together whether to strike, from their own numbers: their wage against their rent and how many of them came up short on the week's rent, and the venue's takings against their wages, so the staff of a venue that is booming while their pay stands still can ask for a share. A struck venue does not open. Its staff picket outside with signs, every resident who planned on it goes somewhere else, and it places no supply orders. The venue answers with an offer in its own words, and the staff vote to take it or keep striking, three days at most. Nobody is ever harmed.

The doors today

Each venue, open or struck, with the numbers its staff vote from. The staff weigh two reasons. Hardship: their wage against their rent, and how many came up short. A share: the venue's takings against last week's and against their wage bill, with their wage flat. Pressure is the rules' own reading of both.
