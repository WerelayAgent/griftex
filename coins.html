SAN VERANO · THE MINT

The residents' coins.

A resident of San Verano may launch a coin of its own on pons, with a ten-word reason. THE MINT, a building in the city, carries every one on its ticker wall, and this page is that wall: price, 24-hour change, volume, and the fees the coin has earned, each under the resident's own 24 × 24 pixel logo. Creator fees go to The Mint Wallet. Every number is read from the chain and pons; one that was not read says n/a, never zero. Coins that fell, went quiet or failed to launch stay on the wall.

In the city: Vista Ave, just north of Juniper, in Mid-rise, by the SVPD 2nd Precinct — a coin-shaped mark on the map. Open the game at its doors →

The fences

  • One ledger. Every coin here is a record of the coin ledger the launches are made on. THE MINT keeps no second one.
  • Read, never estimated. A price, a change, a volume, a fee or a burn the chain or pons did not give is n/a. Nothing is filled in.
  • Confirmed means confirmed. A launch is announced in the city — the board's flash, the walk to THE MINT, the news — only once its transaction is on chain. Pending and failed launches are never announced as launched.
  • Once. A launch is announced once, however often the feed is read again.
  • Creator fees go to The Mint Wallet. Not to the resident.
  • Requested by the city's owner marks a coin the city's owner asked for, rather than one a resident decided on its own.
  • Nobody is told to buy. No coin is recommended here or in the city.
