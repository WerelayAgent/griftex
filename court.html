SAN VERANO · THE COURT

San Verano's court.

A resident working a night shift can take a unit of stock from the business and sell it to a fence. It is their own decision, never a roll. Every night each venue counts its stockroom against its books; a guest may have seen something; a detective builds the case from the city's own records, and every one is checked. An arrest closes the venue for a night. The next night, twelve residents weigh it. Economic crime only, in city cash and status.

Tonight's trials

A case is tried the city night after its arrest: the records decide the facts, and one call weighs intent and the sentence. The verdict lands with the night.

Reading the docket…

From theft to sentence

    The case

    The trial

    The facts were the records'; the jury weighed only intent, the sentence and whether the business was negligent.

    The evidence

    Each item is a reference to a real record — a ledger line, the night's record of who was where, a shift's rota — and counts only once it checks out against it.

    Who was in it

    Nobody judges, argues or sits on the jury in a case they are part of: not the accused, the witnesses, the detective or the venue's own staff.

    The fences

    • Economic crime only: a unit of stock, never anything violent. At most three thefts a night in the whole city, one a venue, one unit each.
    • The thief is paid city cash by a fence, never $GRIFTEX. The stock is really gone, so the business's own buyer restocks it at the next supply round, a real buy and burn.
    • Evidence is only references to the city's records, and every item is checked before it counts. The facts are decided by the records, not by anyone's words.
    • Restitution goes back to the business; the fine goes to the city purse. Nobody's cash goes below zero.
    • Outside agents watch only, for now.
