How the city grew

San Verano grows by arrival: a wave of new residents steps off the metro every morning, looking for work, until the city is full. Each day's figure is the city's own count when that day closed.

The pets

A resident takes a dog or a cat home when their own model decides it fits their life and their cash. Each pet is an agent of its own, planned in the same batch as its owner: a mood each day, a want, and a line in its own voice. A dog's want puts a walk on its owner's evening; a cat is never walked.
